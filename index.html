Welcome to the MF Quant Core Workstation. We rank mutual funds using strict mathematical benchmarks to separate genuine manager skill from lucky market runs. Small and Mid Cap funds are penalized if they become too bloated (hard to manage) or hold overly concentrated stock positions.
⭐ Overall Rating (Max 5★) The fund's final score. It combines Rolling Returns (25%), Information Ratio (25%), CAPM Alpha (25%), and Downside Protection (25%), minus size-based penalties.
📈 3Y Rolling Return Measures consistency. Instead of looking at one date, we average every possible 3-year return over the fund's life. Like measuring a runner's speed over the entire race rather than just the final lap.
⚡ CAPM Alpha (3Y) The manager's value-add. The percentage of returns generated by pure stock-picking skill, after removing the effects of general market movements and risk. Positive is outperformance.
🛡️ Downside Capture Capital protection shield. Measures how much of the market's losses the fund suffers. If the index drops 10% and the fund drops 8%, capture is 80%. Lower is better (protects your capital).
🎯 Information Ratio Skill vs. Luck indicator. Measures consistency in beating the index compared to the extra risk taken. A high ratio proves the manager has true investing skill rather than just hitting a lucky streak.
💡 Note: Since fund managers' official tenure dates are not exposed in standard factsheet APIs, click on any manager's name in the table to instantly lookup their career history, tenure, and background on LinkedIn.
